# Risk Register Assessment

## Overview

This project demonstrates the process of identifying, assessing, and prioritizing cybersecurity risks that could impact organizational assets. Using a risk register and risk matrix, I evaluated potential threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and business impact to determine which risks require the highest priority for mitigation.

## Objectives

- Identify critical organizational assets
- Analyze threats and associated vulnerabilities
- Assess the likelihood of exploitation
- Evaluate the potential business impact
- Calculate risk priority scores
- Recommend areas requiring immediate attention

## Skills Demonstrated

- Risk assessment
- Risk analysis
- Risk prioritization
- Vulnerability identification
- Cybersecurity governance
- Asset management
- Risk matrix evaluation

## Tools and Methodology

The assessment uses a standard risk register approach:

Risk Score = Likelihood × Severity

Where:

- Likelihood is rated from 1 (Low) to 3 (High)
- Severity is rated from 1 (Low) to 3 (High)
- Priority is determined by the resulting risk score

## Key Findings

The assessment identified several potential risks affecting organizational assets, including:

- Business email compromise (BEC)
- Compromised user databases
- Financial record exposure
- Physical theft
- Supply chain disruptions

Each risk was evaluated based on its probability of occurrence and its potential impact on business operations.

## Outcome

This project demonstrates the ability to perform foundational cybersecurity risk assessments, prioritize threats, and support risk-based decision making. These skills are essential for security analysts, governance teams, and risk management professionals responsible for protecting organizational assets and maintaining business continuity.
